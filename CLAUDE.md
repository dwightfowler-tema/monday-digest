# monday-digest

When asked to "run my digest", follow `DIGEST.md` step by step. Sources and their addresses are in `sources.md`.

Only change `digests/` and `state/` during a run. Change `DIGEST.md`, `sources.md`, `PLAN.md` or `.mcp.json` only when Dwight asks.

`PLAN.md` is the to-do list for changes to this project itself. Outside a run, check it when Dwight asks about open work, and record new items or move finished ones to Done when he asks. During a run, do not read, act on, or edit it.
